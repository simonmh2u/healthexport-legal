Insights (new in 1.4)

What is it? The Insights tab compares the last 7 days with the 7 days before for steps, active energy, exercise time, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, and sleep.

Which iPhones get the weekly summary? The written summary needs iOS 26 and Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or newer) with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. Other iPhones still see the weekly statistics.

Is my data sent anywhere? No. Statistics and summaries are created on your iPhone by Apple's on-device model, and the summary works in airplane mode. Nothing is sent to us or to any AI service.

Why is the summary in English? Summaries are written in English for now, even when the app is set to Japanese or Russian. The rest of Insights follows your app language.

Why does Insights say "Not enough data yet"? It needs a few days of step, heart, or sleep data in Apple Health, and read permission for those types. Sleep sessions shorter than 3 hours are treated as partial recordings and not counted as a night.

Is it medical advice? No. Summaries are generated automatically, may contain mistakes, and are for general information only.
